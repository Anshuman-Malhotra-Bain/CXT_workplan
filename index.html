Instructions
  1. Set the day: Pick the day and month from the dropdowns and type the year up top. Want to change the greeting or the line under it? Just click on it and type.
  2. Add a task: Hover over a task bar, click the + that pops up at the top right, and start typing.
  3. Assign people quickly: Just add " - AS/AF" to the end of a task and those initials turn into name icons. Works whether you type it or paste it in.
  4. Prefer dragging? Grab a name icon from "Team today" and drop it onto a task. Or click a name icon, then click each task you want to tag with it. Hit "Done" or press Esc when you're finished.
  5. Took someone off a task? Hover over their icon on the task and click the little ×.
  6. Tick off a task: Click the round dot on its left. It strikes through and vanishes.
  7. Shuffle tasks around: Hover over a task and use the up and down arrows on the right to move it up or down within the bar.
  8. Break a task into sub-points: While you're typing, press Tab to tuck it in as a sub-bullet, or Shift+Tab to pull it back out. You can nest up to two levels.
  9. Talk instead of type: Click into a task, hit the microphone on the right, and start speaking. Click it again to stop. Say "hyphen" for a dash and "slash" for a slash. Your words show up as you speak them, so tidy it up and press Enter.
  10. Add or delete a project bar: Hover near the bottom of the board and click "Add project" to start a new one. To remove a bar, hover over it and click the × at the top right twice (the second click confirms).
  11. Rearrange the bars: Drag a bar using its grip handle, or click its number and type where you want it. Everything else renumbers itself.
  12. Flag who's away: Drop a name icon onto the "On training / out" card. Click the little label next to them to jot down why, like "Sick" or "Vacation." Drag them back to "Team today" once they're around again.
  13. Set the daily game: Click "Fun game for the day" to rename it, then click the grey line underneath to drop in today's game link.
  14. Change who's sharing: Click the initials next to "Shared by" and pop in your own (just two letters).
  15. Send it out as an image: Click Export PNG to grab a picture of the board. In Outlook, paste it straight into the email body rather than dragging it in, and drop the game link in as text so people can still click it.
  16. Save a day or bring one back: Click Export JSON to save the day as a file and keep it as your record. Click Import to load a saved one back in, handy on another computer or when you want to revisit an old day.
  17. No need to hit save: Your board saves itself in this browser, so it'll be right where you left it next time. Just remember it only lives in this browser, so use Export JSON if you need it somewhere else.
